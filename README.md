# Bank Customer Churn Analysis & EDA

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) project analyzing 10,000 bank customer records to identify key drivers of customer attrition using Python.

## 📌 Project Overview
- **Dataset:** Churn_Modelling.csv (10,000 records, 14 features)
- **Tools & Libraries:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Objective:** Identify demographic and financial factors leading to customer churn and provide actionable retention insights.

## 🔍 Key Insights & Analysis
1. **Demographic Risk:** Customers in older age brackets exhibited higher churn rates compared to younger segments.
2. **Geographic Distribution:** Significant churn variation observed across regions (notably higher in Germany compared to France and Spain).
3. **Account Activity:** Active bank members showed notably higher retention rates than inactive members.
